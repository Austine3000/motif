## Verdict

Two requirements remain unsatisfied since the prior audit on 2026-03-12:

1. **SCAF-02** — Next.js scaffold execution. The registry contract and overlay exist, but `scaffold-project.js` has no materialization block for `web-nextjs`. Phase 27 was planned to close this gap but was never executed.

2. **ARUN-03** — PID session store and cleanup harness. `runtime-session.js` provides single-session tracking, but the dedicated `auto-run-session-store.js` and cleanup verification harness from Phase 28 were never implemented.

These are the only two blockers. All other 21 requirements are satisfied, 3 of 5 E2E flows are complete, and cross-phase wiring is solid for everything except the Next.js materialization path.
